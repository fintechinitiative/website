About Us

Our Founders

My name is Vivian Zhong and I am a senior at Hopkins School, New Haven. From a young age, I was taught all about personal finance and the importance of financial literacy. In high school, I started learning economic principles and competed in the NEC. I founded FinTech to inspire young students and help them build their own interest in finance.

My name is Andie Hendley, and I am currently a senior at Hopkins School in New Haven. I first became interested in coding while using websites like Scratch and continued to grow this interest on the robotics team. By learning how to code through various classes and hands-on experience coding a robot, my passion for coding continues to grow, and is something I hope to share with others.

Our Story

From Viv

In the summer of 2024, I traveled to China to teach the first class of our financial literacy program to a group of underserved students and I saw firsthand how important learning finance is from a young age. During the next school year, I founded the FinTech Initiative with our goal to continue teaching financial literacy to make this impact in my own community. I asked Andie if she wanted to help me create a program in New Haven. She agreed and brought the idea of including digital literacy as the two are becoming more and more intertwined. Together, we built our organization to reach as many schools as we can.

01 / OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

To empower young minds with essential financial and digital literacy skills, equipping them with the knowledge to manage money wisely and the technical skills to thrive in a digital world.

Through engaging, hands-on programs, we teach young students the fundamentals of financial responsibility and coding, fostering confidence and future-ready skills.

02 / OUR REACH

Our Impact

100 Students
7 Schools
90 Classes

We have expanded significantly, teaching at more schools and growing our volunteer team. We are currently launching a new business program for middle school students in partnership with FBLA and continue reaching out to more schools and volunteers to broaden our impact.

03 ◎

Who

Students from elementary and middle schools all around New Haven and Connecticut

04 ◇

What

Teach classes in finance (investing, budgeting, basic concepts), coding (principles, typed-out coding languages), and business (entrepreneurial skills, inner workings)

05 ✳

Why

To equip children with life-essential skills from a young age to prepare them for their future in an evolving world
